Example Concepts

Card combinations that spark critical discussion.

Digital Puppeteer

Emotion Recognition Children Subscription Social Isolation Entertain

An educational app that monitors children's emotions through a webcam and adapts lessons in real time. Parents pay for detailed emotional reports and mood tracking over time.

Critical considerations

Privacy, emotional manipulation, data ownership, and impact on authentic learning.

Silent Streets

Ambient Surveillance Citizens Government Funding Safety Risks Monitor

A city-wide network of cameras that scan for “suspicious” behaviour and build profiles for every citizen. Real-time alerts go to authorities based on predictive algorithms.

Critical considerations

Civil liberties, algorithmic bias, who defines “suspicious,” and potential for social control.

MindSync Tutoring

Brain-Computer Interfaces Students Freemium Model Learning Challenges Optimize

A headset that uploads knowledge directly to students’ minds. Basic knowledge is free; advanced subjects need a premium subscription — creating “knowledge inequality.”

Critical considerations

Enhancement ethics, educational equity, authenticity of learning, and social stratification.

EcoCredit Social

Smart Devices Workers Marketplace Commission Information Overload Influence

A social platform where environmental impact becomes currency. Users earn EcoCredits for sustainable choices, spendable on exclusive experiences or products.

Critical considerations

Commodification of responsibility, social pressure, greenwashing, and performative activism.

MemoryMarket

Brain-Computer Interfaces Older Adults Pay-Per-Use Mental Health Support

A service that extracts and stores older adults’ memories, then sells access back to family. Premium packages include AI recreations of deceased relatives.

Critical considerations

Commodification of grief, consent in cognitive decline, authenticity, and data ownership after death.

TruthScore

Emotion Recognition Online Communities Advertising Fraud and Deception Analyze

A platform that uses continuous biometric monitoring to assign “truth scores” to everything users say online. Higher scores unlock premium features and status.

Critical considerations

Surveillance capitalism, who defines “truth,” social stratification, and biometric manipulation.
