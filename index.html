The Speculation Game

Design for the future you fear.

The Speculation Game invites players to prototype possible futures through unexpected combinations of technology, people, problems, strategies, and funding models.

The goal is not to find the best solution, but to explore what happens when technologies are applied in different social contexts. Strange, absurd, or uncomfortable combinations can reveal assumptions, tensions, unintended consequences, and competing values.

Use the cards to imagine a speculative system, build a quick prototype, and use it as a starting point for critical discussion and reflection.

.instructions

  1. Draw one card from each category
  2. Design a speculative system
  3. Build a quick prototype
  4. Pitch how it works
  5. Reflect on risks and consequences

Impossible? → swap one card · Too easy? → draw a Twist
