Design your system

Combine the cards into one speculative idea.
Weird is welcome.

Build a quick prototype

Make something physical with materials at hand.
Use paper, post-its, cardboard, markers, or whatever you have.
Keep it rough, then come back to pitch.

Pitch it

Tell the group how it works.
Who benefits, and who might be harmed?

Reflect together

Use these prompts to dig into the consequences.

  • What is the hidden impact?
  • Can it be used for harm as well as good?
  • What if this became widely adopted?
  • Who benefits? Who is harmed?
  • What assumptions does this make about people?
