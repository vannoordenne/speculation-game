02 · Design

Invent a speculative system from your cards. Strange or uncomfortable combinations are useful.

Your concept

Tie each card into the system. Name benefits and possible harms.

03 · Build

Make a quick prototype: sketch, storyboard, paper mock-up, or short narrative.

04 · Pitch

Explain how it works. Who benefits? Who is harmed? Why would it be used?

05 · Reflect

Discuss benefits, risks, and consequences.

Critical Questions to Ask:

  • What is the hidden impact of your system?
  • Can your system be used for harm as well as good?
  • What would happen if this system became widely adopted?
  • Who benefits from this system? Who is harmed?
  • What assumptions does your design make about users?
