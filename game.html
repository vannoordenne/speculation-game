Design your system

Use the hand above. Strange or uncomfortable combinations are useful.

Tie each card into the system. Name benefits and possible harms.

Build a prototype

Sketch, storyboard, paper mock-up, or short narrative — just enough to discuss.

Work offline or on paper. When you’re ready, continue to pitch.

Pitch

Explain how it works. Who benefits? Who is harmed? Why would it be used?

Reflect

Discuss benefits, risks, and consequences.

Ask

  • What is the hidden impact?
  • Can it be used for harm as well as good?
  • What if this became widely adopted?
  • Who benefits? Who is harmed?
  • What assumptions does this make about users?
