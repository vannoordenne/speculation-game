Step 2: Design Your System

Design your speculative, dystopian system from the combination of your cards. Strange or uncomfortable combinations are useful — they surface assumptions and tensions.

Describe Your Concept:

Be specific about how your system combines the elements from your cards. Consider both positive uses and potential negative implications.

Step 3: Build a Prototype

Build a quick prototype. It can be a sketch, storyboard, paper mock-up, or short narrative — just enough to make the system discussable.

Step 4: Pitch

Pitch the concept and explain how it works. What does it do? Who benefits? Who is harmed? Why would it be used?

Step 5: Reflect

Reflect on the benefits, risks, and consequences. Ask critical questions about the implications and impact of the system.

Critical Questions to Ask:

  • What is the hidden impact of your system?
  • Can your system be used for harm as well as good?
  • What would happen if this system became widely adopted?
  • Who benefits from this system? Who is harmed?
  • What assumptions does your design make about users?
